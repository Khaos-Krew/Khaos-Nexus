## Scope

This production slice extends the existing NPC records into a complete narrative and combat NPC tool with deterministic local generation, relationships, portrait/token handling, import/export, reveal controls, faction/location/quest linkage, encounter insertion, and featured-boss compatibility.

Existing NPC IDs and records remain compatible through additive normalization. The DM remains authoritative and generated NPCs are drafts until explicitly saved.

No paid stat blocks are seeded or reproduced. No Discord categories or campaign channel structures are created. Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain excluded under ADR-008.
