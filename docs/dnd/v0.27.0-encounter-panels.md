## Scope

This production slice adds one persistent Discord combat panel per active encounter binding, boss/featured-combatant health presentation, compact party combat state, and DM-configured current-turn roll buttons.

The DM remains authoritative. Roll buttons use the existing secure dice parser and do not automatically apply damage or healing. Hidden combatants, private notes, hidden HP, blind rolls, bot tokens, and protected credentials are never exposed publicly.

No Discord categories or campaign channel structures are created. Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain excluded under ADR-008.
