## Owner-approved decision

Khaos Nexus may offer an explicit one-click action that provisions one campaign category and a previewed channel template. Existing-resource binding remains supported and the default state creates nothing. Provisioning may happen only after the Owner selects the bot and guild, reviews the exact plan, and confirms it.

## Default template

- `campaign-info` text channel
- `table-chat` text channel
- `character-chat` text channel
- `dice-rolls` text channel
- `session-notes` text channel
- `quests-and-loot` text channel
- `dm-private` restricted text channel
- `game-table` voice channel

The preview may disable or rename optional channels. The category and main campaign channel are required.

## Implementation contract

- Store provisioning records and Discord resource IDs for idempotency.
- Reuse existing managed resources and repair missing managed resources without duplicating the category.
- Batch Discord operations with bounded concurrency, rate-limit handling, cancellation, progress, and structured partial-failure results.
- Bind created resources to the campaign and refresh the persistent campaign panel after the batch completes.
- Do not create Discord roles by default.
- Apply permissions from configured campaign role mappings, explicit member mappings, and registered-bot grants.
- Never delete or rename unrelated resources.
- Cleanup requires a separate confirmation and may affect only resources proven to be Nexus-managed.
- Preserve existing manual IDs and existing-channel, thread, and forum-post workflows.

## UI contract

- Separate `Use Existing Resource` from `Create Campaign Space`.
- Use a readiness step, editable preview step, confirmation step, progress step, and result step.
- Keep manual IDs under an advanced disclosure.
- Show missing bot permissions and missing role mappings before confirmation.
- Do not perform a full D&D workspace rerender for each created channel.
