# Khaos Nexus v0.35.0-B — Navigation and Visual Overhaul

This beta candidate introduces the refreshed Khaos Nexus desktop interface on top of the merged bundled AI runtime and supervised Nexus AI Core foundation.

## Highlights

- Dedicated top-level D&D workspace.
- Dedicated top-level Nexus AI workspace.
- Reorganized navigation with all existing routes and controls preserved.
- Dark red-and-black Khaos Nexus visual system with local SVG backgrounds.
- Reduced-motion support and readable dense-workspace fallbacks.
- Existing updater, diagnostics, permissions, server controls, schedulers, Discord automation, D&D AI isolation, and Nexus AI Core authority boundaries remain unchanged.

## Test focus

- Launch from an installed v0.32.0-B build and detect the beta update.
- Verify installer backup, download, install, restart, and rollback behavior.
- Confirm D&D, Nexus AI, servers, Discord, modules, monitor, logs, settings, updater, and diagnostics remain reachable.
- Confirm sidebar and workspace panes scroll independently.
- Confirm no provider credentials or service tokens appear in renderer state, logs, diagnostics, backups, or visual assets.

## Safety boundaries

- Nexus AI Core remains advisory-only.
- D&D AI remains the only AI service allowed to receive explicitly approved campaign context.
- Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain paused and excluded from the Windows package.
- No automatic maintenance, moderation, public announcements, or campaign changes are introduced.
