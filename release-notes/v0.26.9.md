# Khaos Nexus v0.26.9-B — D&D Draft Refresh Guard

This beta hotfix keeps the startup-safe `v0.26.8-B` application baseline and fixes the D&D workspace refresh behavior that could flicker and replace unsaved selections before Save.

## Fixed

- Unsaved D&D inputs, dropdowns, checkboxes, and text are protected from background workspace replacement.
- Fifteen-second polling and live D&D updates continue receiving data without replacing an active dirty form.
- Campaign and tab navigation are blocked while edits are unsaved unless the Owner explicitly discards them.
- Dirty state clears only after a successful D&D mutation. Failed saves preserve the current draft.
- Equivalent workspace renders are ignored to reduce unnecessary flicker.
- A visible notice appears when newer campaign data is waiting behind protected edits.

## Startup safety

- No new main-process startup extension was added.
- The existing startup entry path from `v0.26.8-B` remains unchanged.
- Packaged Windows validation must reach startup phase `ready` with `limitedMode=false` before publication.
- The two earlier startup-breaking draft-preservation implementations remain excluded.

## Rollback

The immediate rollback is `v0.26.8-B`. Stable fallback remains `v0.26.0`.

Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain paused and excluded under ADR-008.
