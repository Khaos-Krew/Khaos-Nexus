- Keeps Command Center pinned at the top.
- Organizes workspaces into Servers, Discord & Community, Automation, Modules & Tools, and System.
- Adds a workspace search box.
- Keeps only one menu group expanded at a time.
- Automatically places future module pages into a suitable group.
- Detects and hides duplicate navigation entries without removing their workspaces.
- Preserves independent sidebar and workspace scrolling.

- Retains the mandatory verified pre-update backup.
