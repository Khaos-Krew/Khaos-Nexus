# Khaos Nexus v0.26.6-B

This beta hotfix protects unsaved D&D form edits from the workspace's live background refresh cycle.

## Fixed

- Prevents the 15-second D&D refresh from replacing an actively edited workspace.
- Stops unsaved text, selections, checkboxes, focus, editor state, and scroll position from snapping back to stored defaults before Save.
- Queues newer campaign data behind an unsaved draft instead of silently overwriting the form.
- Adds a visible choice to keep editing or discard the draft and apply the latest data.
- Prevents accidental campaign or tab navigation while unsaved edits are protected.
- Preserves normal campaign switching when no draft exists.
- Restores the encounter-panel stability wrapper required for auto-refresh re-enablement and simultaneous HP/maximum-HP updates.

## Included beta features

This build retains the v0.26.5-beta D&D feature set:

- signed free-content catalog and character imports;
- protected local campaign maps and markers;
- expanded NPC builder, generator, relationships, and encounter integration;
- persistent Discord encounter panels and DM-configured turn actions.

## Safety and compatibility

- Existing campaigns, characters, settings, credentials, and local files are preserved.
- The installer performs the existing verified pre-update backup.
- Immediate beta rollback: `v0.26.5-beta`.
- Stable fallback: `v0.26.0`.
- Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain paused and excluded under ADR-008.
- The unfinished visual UI refresh is not included.
