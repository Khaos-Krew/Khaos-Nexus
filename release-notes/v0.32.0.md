# Khaos Nexus v0.32.0-B

## Test candidate

This beta candidate adds the isolated dual-AI service foundation for the Khaos Nexus desktop application.

### Added

- Central **AI Services** workspace for configuring D&D AI and Nexus AI Core independently.
- Separate protected endpoints, service tokens, health state, capability discovery, and audit boundaries.
- Nexus AI Core v0.7.0 integration pinned to commit `300c653e5643e0ee2e15590f8cb53e30ee7a79ff`.
- Optional primary Nexus Bot linkage for authenticated health and capability checks only.
- Explicit rejection of D&D capabilities and campaign context through Nexus AI Core.
- Secret redaction and backup exclusions for both AI service tokens.

### Preserved boundaries

- D&D AI remains the only AI service allowed to receive approved campaign context.
- Nexus AI Core cannot publish to Discord, run game-server commands, schedule work, install updates, or perform maintenance actions in this candidate.
- Registered secondary and Bring Your Own Bot runtimes do not receive Nexus AI Core credentials.
- Existing updater backup, diagnostics, scheduler, Discord, D&D, and game-server controls remain authoritative.
- Android Companion and Mobile Gateway remain paused and excluded from production under ADR-008.
- No APK or Android setup link is included in this Windows desktop release.

### Test focus

- Install or update from v0.31.0-B to v0.32.0-B through the in-app updater.
- Confirm the application restarts and reports version `0.32.0-B`.
- Open **AI Services** and verify D&D AI and Nexus AI Core appear separately.
- Save and test each service independently.
- Confirm disabling either service does not affect the other service or normal Nexus Bot controls.
- Confirm tokens never appear in logs, diagnostics, backups, renderer state, or registered-bot configuration.

This candidate is intended for controlled owner testing before broader publication.
